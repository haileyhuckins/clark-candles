Products

Discover Our Handcrafted Soy Candles
At Clark Candles LLC, we believe every candle should bring warmth, comfort, and a little something special to your home. Our handcrafted soy candles are made with care and attention to detail, offering a beautiful addition to any room or a thoughtful gift for someone you love.

Soy wax is derived from soybean oil and is commonly used as an alternative to traditional paraffin wax. Our candles are made to order, allowing us to focus on each product individually and provide a personal touch. Whether you enjoy relaxing after a long day, creating a cozy atmosphere, or adding a welcoming fragrance to your space, our candles are designed to complement your everyday moments.

Customers can explore a variety of fragrance options and personalized designs to create a candle that suits their preferences, décor, or special occasion. Each candle is carefully prepared and packaged, making it a wonderful choice for birthdays, holidays, celebrations, or simply treating yourself.

Because our products are handcrafted, small variations in appearance may occur, making each candle unique. We are proud to be a family-owned small business in San Dimas, California, and appreciate every customer who supports our passion for handmade products. Thank you for choosing Clark Candles LLC, where every candle is made with care and created to brighten your space.

Handcrafted with care — made just for you, one candle at a time.
